## ⏪ Recap of Last Session

### 🌊 The Descent into the Indigo Murk

After dealing with vicious harpies in Key Landing and gathering some choice arcane components—including exactly **20 harpy feathers** plucked by **Everywhere**—the party set their sights on the pirate map. Leaving the relative safety of the _Twilight Drift_, you dove 300 feet down into the bitingly cold, geothermal-starved waters off Sunware Key.

Before you could even reach the wreckage, **four Giant Skeletal Sharks** with glowing green ribcages charged out of the gloom. After turning the undead apex predators into scattered bone meal, you breached the fractured stern of the **_Tammeraut_**, driven into the silt like a splintered spike. Inside, the vertical maze of collapsed decks was choked with black muck and stained by an eerie, pulsing multi-colored light.

### 💀 Battle in the Pit of Hatred

Navigating past a stray **Chuul**—which **Harvin** brilliantly neutralized by convincing local **giant coral snakes** to join the attack—the party descended directly into the **Pit of Hatred**. There, a nauseatingly radiant rift to the Underworld lay guarded by the drowned pirate sorcerer **Captain Syrgaul**, **four towering Boneclaws**, and a **Mysterious Skeletal Sorcerer**.

During the chaotic, high-stakes battle, Syrgaul taunted the party with chilling accuracy, echoing a terrifying truth: **you are all being cultivated.**

- He mocked **Harvin’s** connection to Athreos, claiming his master Phenax had woven a shroud the Ferryman couldn't see through.
    
- He called **Everywhere** a "ripening power" and questioned if Heliod's light was a brand of ownership.
    
- He demanded **Estrid** bleed for them, claiming her royal Underworld lineage was the exact soil Phenax's "Divine Seeds" required to grow.
    

### 🩸 Casualties and Consequences

The party fought with legendary ferocity, ultimately shattering Syrgaul's forces and completing the fated book, but victory demanded a steep toll:

- **The Fallen Cleric:** **Janore**, Cleric of Thassa, tragically lost her life. After the battle, the crew constructed a casket, weighted it down with cannonballs, and gave her a honorable burial at sea.
    
- **The Soul-Blight:** Both **Everywhere** and **Alister** contracted **Bluerot** from Syrgaul's decaying magic.
    
- **The Massive Loose End:** In the rush to secure the ship, treat the infected, and send word back to Key Landing, the party left the **Portal to Hell completely open** in the bedrock of the Pit.
