## ⏪ Recap of Last Session
### **The Departure from Val Taruum**

The session began with the party tying up loose ends in the city before their long journey north.
- **Harvin’s Preparations**: Harvin coordinated the delivery of bags containing skeletons and a sending stone to the ship.
- **The Altar of Heliod**: Everywhere moved forward with his goal of honoring the Bright Father. He paid Norwan Valedale 52 GP to begin construction of an altar at the end of Pier 7A in the Merchant Mile. A special "anchor brick" engraved with the ship's name was laid in the foundation.
- **Royal Support**: King Grimbeard delivered the promised monthly stipend of 5 tons of Powdered Sordalite Fuel and assigned Foreman Doydown to begin building barracks on the pier.
- **The Ancient Smith**: While visiting the obsidian hut of the Ancient Smith to discuss the repair of the Moss Wall, the smith provided a cryptic warning: the King must be reminded that repairs require entry into the "world break" and a price must be paid.
### **Shadows and Bargains**

A significant portion of the session involved a tense negotiation with **Lady Ebonshade** and the Shadowed Covenant.
- **The Meeting**: The party traveled to the "Nexus of Whispers," a clandestine base hidden within the structural tunnels of the Unification Bridge's Low Ward.
- **The Deal**: Lady Ebonshade offered intelligence on the Night Lord (Raunaeril Xistrith) and an **Amulet of Proof Against Detection and Location** to shield Estrid from her father’s senses. In exchange, she demanded a vial of Estrid’s blood.
- **The Outcome**: Estrid initially refused the deal. However, as an act of "good faith," Lady Ebonshade left the magical amulet under Harvin’s pillow.
### **Arrival at Sunware Key**

The party boarded the _Twilight Drift_ for a 40-hour flight to the northern reaches.

- **The Landscape**: Upon arrival, they found Sunware Key to be a 50-mile island dominated by an active volcano. The geothermal activity creates a strangely warm "paradise" amidst the freezing northern seas.
- **Key Landing**: They observed the village of Key Landing, a settlement of 900 souls with a harbor disproportionately large for its size, filled with trading and naval vessels.
- **The Fortress**: Off the coast, they spotted a small stone fortress with a bell tower and gardens, nestled between three stony peaks on a rugged islet known as Firewatch Island.
- **The Harpies**: Upon arriving to Sunware Key the crew went to the town of Uskarn. At the Fog and Frog the party heard a commotion from the outside. They were then vicisously attacked and sung to by harpies before killing them all and saving Alister from following one to his doom.

### **Unresolved Threads**

- **The Sword**: The party’s custom sword is expected to be finished in one week.
- **The Quest for Essence**: Everywhere learned that the price of a magic ring could be significantly dropped (from 4000 GP) if he provides the bottled essence of an incorporeal creature.
- **The Next Move**: The party must now decide whether to follow the pirate's map toward the wreck of the _Tammeraut_ or any other affairs on Sunware Key.
- **Estrid's Love Life**: What was Estrid doing with Xander when Harvin went to deliver the amulet to her?

### 2. The Approach
As you descend further, the indigo murk gives way to a sight of haunting desolation. The rotting skeleton of the _Tammeraut’s_ stern looms up from the ocean floor, a jagged monument to the ship's violent end. The vessel clearly broke in half during its final plunge; while the bow is nowhere to be seen, the stern section struck the seafloor with such force that it remains driven into the silt like a massive, splintered spike.

The sandy bed surrounding the wreckage is a chaotic field of partially buried bones and waterlogged debris. As you draw closer, the geothermal warmth of Sunware Key's volcanic activity vanishes entirely. The water becomes unnaturally, bitingly cold, and the vibrant schools of fish you saw near the surface are conspicuously absent here, as if the living instinctively shun this place.

It is in this dead zone that the rhythmic flashes of white first appear. Emerging from the gloom are four **Giant Skeletal Sharks**. Their thirty-foot frames are stripped of flesh, leaving only yellowed bone held together by dark necromantic energy. Their empty ribcages pulse with a sickly green light. With terrifying speed, they snap their cavernous, tooth-filled jaws and dive toward you, their hollow forms silent and deadly in the crushing pressure 300 feet below.

### 2.5. The Wreck
As the party pushes past the skeletal remains of the sharks and enters the fractured stern of the _Tammeraut_, the environment shifts from the open cold of the abyss to a claustrophobic, decaying tomb.

##### **The Shattered Decks**

The interior is a treacherous ruin where the once-grand war galley has been reduced to a vertical maze of splinters. Because the ship is spiked into the seafloor, "down" now leads toward the dark heart of the vessel.

- **A Tangled Labyrinth**: The decks have almost entirely collapsed, leaving a chaotic web of sundered timbers and jagged beams that snag on gear and slow movement.
    
- **The Corruption of the Deep**: Thick layers of ooze and black muck cling to every surface, obscuring the path and muffling sound.
    
- **The Absence of Life**: There is no movement here—no crabs, no small fish, and no swaying kelp. The only thing that moves is the silt disturbed by your passage.
    

##### **The Pulsing Light**

As you navigate deeper into the wreckage, a surreal visual phenomenon begins to dominate the darkness.

- **The Many-Colored Pulse**: From the lightless depths of the lower hull, an eerie, multi-colored light begins to pulse in a slow, rhythmic fashion.
    
- **Chromatic Distortion**: The light doesn't illuminate the wreckage so much as it stains it, casting sickly greens, deep purples, and bruised reds across the rotting wood.
    
- **The Divine Seed**: This light seems to resonate with the presence of the **Deceiver**, signaling that you are nearing the source of the necromantic energy—a place where the boundaries between the sea and the Underworld are dangerously thin.
    

##### **Approaching the Pit of Hatred**

The descent leads toward the lowest point of the stern, where the pressure feels heavier and the "many-colored light" is at its brightest.

- **The Smell of Decay**: Even through water, a sense of "Envy and Misfortune" permeates the area, a hallmark of **Erebos’s** influence over the treasures of the dead.
    
- **The Final Gate**: The sundered beams begin to form a more deliberate shape, narrowing into a jagged opening that leads directly into the **Pit of Hatred**. Below this threshold, the silt gives way to a deeper, unnatural cavern where the captain, Syrgaul, awaits his "Divine Seeds" to fulfill the Deceiver's final goal.

### 3. The Pit of Hatred

##### **The Atmosphere of the Pit**

The "many-colored light" seen from the decks above is now a blinding, nauseating radiance that permeates the entire fifteen-foot-high underwater cavern. This light emanates from a funnel-shaped hollow in a northern alcove, where a narrow rift in the bedrock serves as a gateway to something far more ancient.

The seafloor here is a macabre landscape—a hideous array of piled bones, many of which are clearly humanoid. The sheer volume suggests that Syrgaul has been harvesting the "Treasures of the Dead" from more than just his own sunken crew, perhaps as an offering to **Erebos**, the Underking.

##### **The Battle Array**

Standing amidst the bones and the pulsing light are the guardians of the rift:

- **Syrgaul, Captain of the Tammeraut**: The pirate sorcerer stands at the center, his form a terrifying blend of drowned rot and magical authority.
    
- **The Boneclaws**: Four towering, skeletal horrors with elongated, needle-like talons lurk in the shadows of the sundered timbers, ready to strike at any "Divine Seed" that approaches.
    
- **The Mysterious Skeletal Sorcerer**: Standing directly beside the pulsing rift, this figure seems to be the conduit for the **Deceiver's** influence, maintaining the spell that threatens to "upset the balance of the pantheon".
